The problem
02 / 07

Banks lend against progress they cannot see.

Construction loans are paid in tranches. Before each one, the bank has to answer one question — what has actually been built? Today the answer is assembled by hand.

Capital at risk

Paying for work that is not there

Money released against unverified progress is hard to get back. The bank carries the exposure until the building is finished — or is not.

176.1mln UZS asked above what the expert confirmed, on one tranche (sample data)
Time

Days lost on every tranche

Document checks, site visits and sign-off each wait on someone else. Every day of delay is a day the developer is not building.

5 stepsper tranche, each waiting on someone else: documents, inspection, decision, sign-off
Accountability

No defensible record

When a loan is reviewed, who approved what — and on what evidence — has to be rebuilt from inboxes.

4 sourcesreconciled by hand for every request: budget, invoices, site photos, correspondence
