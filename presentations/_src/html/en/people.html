Four people. One tranche. No guesswork.

Each person does their part in the same place and sees what the others did.

01
Developer

Asks for the next part

Picks the work item, enters the amount, attaches photos and documents.

02
Expert

Checks the site

Visits the building and records how much is actually done.

03
Bank specialist

Decides line by line

Pays for confirmed work and holds back what is missing.

04
Second approver

Signs off

Nobody can approve a decision they prepared themselves.

What it means for you

If you are buying a home

The bank financing your building pays only for work confirmed on site — and sees early when the schedule starts slipping.

If you build

You know which documents are needed, where your request stands and its deadline. You fix only the line in dispute.

If you inspect

You get a clear task, record what is done on site, and your findings reach the bank directly.

If you work at a bank

Everything a decision needs is on one card — and the decision stays on record.
