Decision
04 / 07

Decide line by line. Sign off with a quorum.

The amount is calculated

% complete × plan − paid before. More than the confirmed amount is refused.

Six ways to decide

Approve, issue a part, return, reject, order a control measurement or suspend financing.

Exceptions need a reason

Saved in the journal. An exception to a blocking rule goes to the committee.

The route builds itself

Expert → specialist → head of department → committee when the amount is above the threshold.
