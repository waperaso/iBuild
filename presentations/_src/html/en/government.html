Where iBuild fits
06 / 07

Works alongside state systems. Replaces none.

The state

Permits and safety

  • State platforms such as Shaffof Qurilish confirm a project’s legality, permits and the developer’s status.
  • State construction supervision checks safety and building norms.
The bank, with iBuild

Money and credit risk

  • Every tranche checked against the bank’s own rules.
  • Inspections with progress, risk and a payout recommendation.
  • A full record of who decided what, and why.
People on site

Quality and judgement

  • Engineers judge concrete, rebar and hidden works; iBuild records their findings.
  • Every payout is decided by a bank employee, never automatically.
